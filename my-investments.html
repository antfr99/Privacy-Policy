Privacy policy

Last updated 14 October 2026. This policy covers the My Investments app. The developer, MyThoughtsAIme Apps, is responsible for your information.

Without an account

You can use the whole app, except posting in Community, without signing in. Your settings, situation, stances, journal and watchlist are kept on this phone. Your own API keys are kept only on this phone in the encrypted storage of Android (Keystore) or iOS (Keychain).

To show its cards, the app sends the server what a card needs — tickers, amounts you typed and the chosen data source. If you added API keys, a key travels with the request in a header over HTTPS, is used for that request only and is never written to disk, a database, the cache or the logs.

If you sign in

  • Email address — to send you a one-time sign-in code. It is never shown to other members.
  • Display name (handle) — shown next to your ratings and replies.
  • What you post — ratings, comments, replies, endorsements and reports.
  • Your journal and watchlist names — copied to your account so they follow you between phones. The quantities you own stay on this phone.

Technical data

Like any web service, the server and its hosting provider keep short-lived request logs (time, address requested, IP address) to run and protect the service. The app checks Expo’s update service for new versions, which sees the app version and the request’s IP address, and the download buttons in About check GitHub, where the install files are kept.

What the app does not do

  • No advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and no selling or renting of your information.
  • No access to your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • It does not link to or read any brokerage or bank account.

Who processes it, and where

Supabase
Sign-in, sign-in emails and the Community, journal and watchlist database — EU (Ireland).
Render
Hosts the My Investments server — EU (Frankfurt).
Expo (EAS Update)
Delivers app updates — United States.

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Database rules let each person read and change only their own journal and watchlist; Community posts are public by design.

How long it is kept

Account data is kept until you delete your account. Deleting it removes your email address, public name, posts, replies, endorsements, reports, blocks and synced journal and watchlist at once. Server logs are kept for a short time by the hosting provider and then removed.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete any rating or reply you posted, from Community, at any time.
  • Remove watchlist names (long-press) and delete journal entries; when you are signed in, the change follows you to your other phones.
  • Delete your account at any time: Community → your account → Delete my account, or on the web at antfr99.github.io/Privacy-Policy/delete-account.html. The account is shared with My Entertainment and Our Timeline, so it is deleted there too.
  • Block anyone from their post’s ⋯ menu; their posts and replies are hidden from you.
  • Sign out at any time; what is on the phone stays on the phone.
  • Ask for a copy of your data or a correction, or for help deleting your account, by writing from the email address you signed in with to mythoughtsaime@gmail.com.

You have rights under South Africa’s Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), and under the GDPR if you are in the EU or UK, including access, correction, deletion and objection. You can complain to South Africa’s Information Regulator or your local data-protection authority.

Community rules

Before your first post you are asked to agree to these rules. Members’ ratings are their own opinions.

  • Be respectful. No harassment, hate, threats or personal attacks.
  • No market manipulation: no pump-and-dump, coordinated buying or selling, or false claims meant to move a price.
  • No inside information, and nothing you are not allowed to share.
  • No spam, advertising, referral links or paid promotion.
  • Don’t impersonate anyone or post anyone’s personal information.
  • Ratings are opinions, not advice — don’t present them as guaranteed outcomes.

Reporting and moderation

Anyone signed in can report a post, or block its author so their posts and replies no longer show for them, from the post’s ⋯ menu. A post reported by three different members is hidden from everyone but its author. The developer reviews reports and can remove posts and accounts that break these rules. To raise anything else, write to mythoughtsaime@gmail.com.
