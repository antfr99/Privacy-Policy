Privacy policy

Last updated 13 October 2026. This policy covers the Our Timeline app. The developer, MyThoughts Apps, is responsible for your information.

Kept on this phone only

Your ratings of each side, your Hindsight answers, what you make of the AI's lines, your thoughts, and your lines and drafts are stored on this phone and never sent anywhere — unless you post a line or an experience, or share something yourself.

If you sign in

  • Email address — to send you a one-time sign-in code. It is never shown to other people.
  • Public name (handle) — shown next to your lines, experiences and comments. It is the same account and name as in the developer's other apps (My Investments, the Investment Playbook and My Entertainment), if you use them.
  • Lines you post — the topic, question, steps (years, what happens, positions, odds and reasons), the first sign it's wrong, the case against, your note, the date, and later your marks on which steps happened.
  • Experiences you post — how it went (very bad to very good), the area (the Office, Everyday Life, AI Coding…), the AI tool if you name it, what happened, and the date.
  • Comments — the text, your stance (agree, partly, disagree or none) and the date.
  • "Thoughtful" and "helpful" marks and reports — which line, experience or comment you marked or reported. Only counts are shown to others.
  • Blocks — whom you blocked. Private to you.
  • When you agreed to the Social rules.

Posted lines, experiences and comments are public: anyone with the app can read them, signed in or not, with your public name. One hidden after reports stays visible to its author.

Technical data

The app checks Expo's update service for new versions, which sees the app version and the request's IP address, and the download buttons in About check GitHub, where the install files are kept. Links you open go to their own websites.

What the app does not do

  • No advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and no selling or renting of your information.
  • No access to your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Who processes it, and where

Supabase
Sign-in, sign-in emails, public names, lines, experiences, comments, marks and reports — EU (Ireland).
Expo (EAS Update)
Delivers app updates — United States.

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Database rules let people post only as themselves, and a posted line or experience can't be changed — only deleted, or marked.

How long it is kept

Account data, lines, experiences and comments are kept until you delete them or delete your account, which removes everything linked to it at once. What is on your phone stays until you delete it or remove the app.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete any line, experience or comment you posted, from Social, at any time. Delete comments on your own lines.
  • Sign out at any time; everything on the phone stays.
  • Delete your account at any time: Social → Delete my account (or About), or on the web at antfr99.github.io/Privacy-Policy/delete-account.html. It removes your account and everything linked to it, here and in the developer's other apps, which share it.
  • Block anyone from their line, comment or experience; what they post is hidden from you.
  • Ask for a copy of your data or a correction, or for help deleting your account, by writing from the email address you signed in with to frigug@gmail.com.

You have rights under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), and under the GDPR if you are in the EU or UK, including access, correction, deletion and objection. You can complain to South Africa's Information Regulator or your local data-protection authority.

Children

The app is meant for adults (18 and over) and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes and the new version appears here.

Social rules

Before your first line, experience or comment you are asked to agree to these rules.

  • Argue with ideas, not people. Disagreement is welcome; harassment, hate, threats and personal attacks are not.
  • Disagree well: say which step you doubt and why.
  • Share your own experiences of AI, good and bad, as they happened. Leave out other people's names and private details.
  • No spam, advertising, referral links or paid promotion.
  • Don't impersonate anyone or post anyone's personal information.
  • No sexual content, no fake images of real people, and nothing illegal.

Moderation

You can delete your own lines, experiences and comments, and comments on your own lines. Report anything that breaks these rules — three reports from different people hide a line, an experience or a comment — and block anyone whose posts you don’t want to see. The developer reviews reports and can remove posts and accounts that break these rules; for anything else, write to frigug@gmail.com.
