Privacy policy

Last updated 13 October 2026. This policy covers the My Entertainment app. The developer, MyThoughts Apps, is responsible for your information.

Without an account

You can use the whole app, except Social, without signing in. Your ♥ list and your Food for thought notebook are kept on this phone only and are never sent anywhere.

If you sign in

  • Email address — to send you a one-time sign-in code. It is never shown to other people.
  • Public name (handle) — shown to friends next to your recommendations. It is the same account and name as in the developer’s other apps (My Investments, the Investment Playbook and Our Timeline), if you use them.
  • Your recommendations — the item, its title, your note (up to 280 characters), who you sent it to and when.
  • Reports and blocks — which recommendation you reported and whom you blocked. They are private.
  • When you agreed to the Social rules.

Signed-in people can see each other’s public names. A recommendation is seen by the person you sent it to, or by everyone signed in if you send it to all friends.

Technical data

Cover images are loaded from TMDB, Open Library, RAWG, Deezer and Wikipedia, which see the request’s IP address like any website. The app checks Expo’s update service for new versions, which sees the app version and the request’s IP address, and the download buttons in About check GitHub, where the install files are kept.

What the app does not do

  • No advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and no selling or renting of your information.
  • No access to your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Who processes it, and where

Supabase
Sign-in, sign-in emails, public names and recommendations — EU (Ireland).
Expo (EAS Update)
Delivers app updates — United States.

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Database rules let each person read only the recommendations sent to them, sent to everyone, or sent by them.

How long it is kept

Account data and recommendations are kept until you delete them or delete your account, which removes everything linked to it at once.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete any recommendation you sent or received, from Social, at any time.
  • Sign out at any time; your ♥ list stays on the phone.
  • Delete your account at any time: Social → Delete my account (or About), or on the web at antfr99.github.io/Privacy-Policy/delete-account.html. It removes your account and everything linked to it, here and in the developer’s other apps, which share it.
  • Block anyone from a recommendation they sent you; you no longer see what they send.
  • Ask for a copy of your data or a correction, or for help deleting your account, by writing from the email address you signed in with to frigug@gmail.com.

You have rights under South Africa’s Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), and under the GDPR if you are in the EU or UK, including access, correction, deletion and objection. You can complain to South Africa’s Information Regulator or your local data-protection authority.

Children

The app is meant for adults (18 and over) and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes and the new version appears here.

Social rules

Before your first recommendation you are asked to agree to these rules.

  • Be respectful. No harassment, hate, threats or personal attacks in your notes.
  • No spam, advertising, referral links or paid promotion.
  • Don’t impersonate anyone or post anyone’s personal information.
  • No spoilers without a warning.

Moderation

Tap ⋯ on a recommendation sent to you to report it or to block the person who sent it; blocked people’s recommendations no longer reach you. Three reports from different people hide a recommendation. You can also delete any recommendation sent to you. The developer reviews reports and can remove recommendations and accounts that break these rules; for anything else, write to frigug@gmail.com.
