Delete your account

The three MyThoughtsAIme Apps share one optional account. Deleting it removes the account and everything linked to it from all three apps, at once and for good.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in email address and public name.
  • My Investments: your friends and friend requests, the stock recommendations you sent and received and every reply in those conversations (for both people), your reports, your synced journal and watchlist, and anything left from the old public Community tab.
  • My Entertainment: your friends and friend requests, the recommendations you sent and received and every reply in those conversations (for both people), and your reports.
  • Our Timeline: your friends and friend requests, the recommendations you sent and received and every reply in those conversations (for both people), your reports, and anything left from the old public Social tab.
  • Your blocks and your agreement to the Recommendation rules.

Nothing is kept after deletion, except that a report someone else made keeps its copy of the reported words — no longer linked to your account — while it is reviewed. What an app stored only on your phone (settings, notes, drafts, API keys) stays there until you uninstall the app.

Delete it in the app

Open any of the apps, sign in, and tap Delete my account — under your account on the Recommendations tab (Recs in My Investments), or in About.

Delete it here

No app needed. We email you a one-time code to prove the account is yours.

Or ask by email

Write to mythoughtsaime@gmail.com from the address you sign in with, asking for your account to be deleted. It will be deleted within 30 days and you’ll get a reply when it’s done.
