Privacy policy

Last updated 15 October 2026. This policy covers the My Entertainment app. The developer, MyThoughtsAIme Apps, is responsible for your information.

Without an account

You can use the whole app, except Recommendations, without signing in. Your ♥ list and your Food for thought notebook are kept on this phone only and are never sent anywhere.

If you sign in

  • Email address — to send you a one-time sign-in code. It is never shown to other people.
  • Public name (handle) — found by people searching for friends, and shown to your friends next to your recommendations and replies. It is the same account and name as in the developer’s other apps (My Investments, the Investment Playbook and Our Timeline), if you use them.
  • Your friends — whom you asked or were asked by, and when a request was accepted.
  • Your recommendations and replies — the item, its title, your note (up to 280 characters) and replies (up to 500), who you sent them to and when.
  • Reports and blocks — what or whom you reported, the reason, a copy of the reported words, and whom you blocked. They are private: only the developer sees reports.
  • When you agreed to the Recommendations rules.

Signed-in people can find each other by public name, to send a friend request. Nothing else about you is shown to anyone. A recommendation, and the replies to it, are seen only by the two friends in it; there is no way to send to everyone.

Technical data

Cover images are loaded from TMDB, Open Library, RAWG, Deezer and Wikipedia, which see the request’s IP address like any website. The app checks Expo’s update service for new versions, which sees the app version and the request’s IP address, and the download buttons in About check GitHub, where the install files are kept.

What the app does not do

  • No advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and no selling or renting of your information.
  • No access to your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Who processes it, and where

Supabase
Sign-in, sign-in emails, public names and recommendations — EU (Ireland).
Expo (EAS Update)
Delivers app updates — United States.

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Database rules let each person read only their own friendships and the recommendations and replies they are part of, and only let friends who have accepted each other send them.

How long it is kept

Account data and recommendations are kept until you delete them or delete your account, which removes everything linked to it at once.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete any conversation you are in, or any reply you wrote, from Recommendations, at any time. Remove a friend or decline a request at any time.
  • Sign out at any time; your ♥ list stays on the phone.
  • Delete your account at any time: Recommendations → Delete my account (or About), or on the web at mythoughtsaime.github.io/Privacy-Policy/delete-account.html. It removes your account and everything linked to it, here and in the developer’s other apps, which share it.
  • Block anyone — from a friend, a request, a recommendation or a reply. They can no longer send you requests, recommendations or replies, and what they sent disappears from your view.
  • Ask for a copy of your data or a correction, or for help deleting your account, by writing from the email address you signed in with to mythoughtsaime@gmail.com.

You have rights under South Africa’s Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), and under the GDPR if you are in the EU or UK, including access, correction, deletion and objection. You can complain to South Africa’s Information Regulator or your local data-protection authority.

Children

The app is meant for adults (18 and over) and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes and the new version appears here.

Recommendations rules

Before your first friend request, recommendation or reply you are asked to agree to these rules.

  • Be respectful. No harassment, hate, threats or personal attacks in your notes or replies.
  • Only add people you know, and don’t keep asking someone who declined.
  • No spam, advertising, links or paid promotion (links are blocked).
  • Don’t impersonate anyone or post anyone’s personal information.
  • No spoilers without a warning.

Moderation

You can only be sent anything by friends you have accepted. Tap ⋯ beside a friend, a request or a recommendation — or press and hold a reply — to remove a friend, block them or report them, the recommendation or the reply. Removing a friend stops new recommendations and replies between you; blocking also stops friend requests and hides what they sent, here and in the developer’s other apps. Every report goes to the developer, who reviews it and can remove content and accounts that break these rules; for anything else, write to mythoughtsaime@gmail.com.
