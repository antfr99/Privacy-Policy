Privacy policy

Last updated 15 October 2026. This policy covers the My Investments app. The developer, MyThoughtsAIme Apps, is responsible for your information.

Without an account

You can use the whole app, except Recommendations, without signing in. Your settings, situation, stances, journal and watchlist are kept on this phone. Your own API keys are kept only on this phone in the encrypted storage of Android (Keystore) or iOS (Keychain).

To show its cards, the app sends the server what a card needs — tickers, amounts you typed and the chosen data source. If you added API keys, a key travels with the request in a header over HTTPS, is used for that request only and is never written to disk, a database, the cache or the logs.

If you sign in

  • Email address — to send you a one-time sign-in code. It is never shown to other members.
  • Public name (handle) — what friends search for to add you, shown on your recommendations and replies. Anyone signed in can look a name up; nothing else about you is shown.
  • Your friends — who you asked, who asked you and who accepted. Only you and that person can see it.
  • Recommendations and replies — the stock, your note and the conversation. Only the two people in it can read them.
  • Reports you make — the reason, anything you add, and a copy of what you reported, for the developer to review.
  • Your journal and watchlist names — copied to your account so they follow you between phones. The quantities you own stay on this phone.

Technical data

Like any web service, the server and its hosting provider keep short-lived request logs (time, address requested, IP address) to run and protect the service. The app checks Expo’s update service for new versions, which sees the app version and the request’s IP address, and the download buttons in About check GitHub, where the install files are kept.

What the app does not do

  • No advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and no selling or renting of your information.
  • No access to your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone, and no notifications.
  • It does not link to or read any brokerage or bank account.

Who processes it, and where

Supabase
Sign-in, sign-in emails and the database for friends, recommendations, journal and watchlist — EU (Ireland).
Render
Hosts the My Investments server — EU (Frankfurt).
Expo (EAS Update)
Delivers app updates — United States.

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Database rules let each person read and change only their own journal and watchlist, and let a recommendation and its replies be read only by the two friends in it. Nothing in Recommendations is public. Recommendations are not end-to-end encrypted: like any server database, they are readable by the developer when reviewing a report or a legal request.

How long it is kept

Account data is kept until you delete your account. Deleting it removes your email address, public name, friends, recommendations, replies, blocks and synced journal and watchlist at once (in older versions also any Community ratings). Reports you made go too; a report someone else made about you keeps its copy of the reported words, no longer linked to your account, while it is reviewed. Server logs are kept for a short time by the hosting provider and then removed.

Your choices and rights

  • Withdraw any recommendation you sent, or delete any reply you wrote, at any time — it goes for both people.
  • Remove a friend at any time: no new recommendations or replies either way. Block someone to stop all contact and hide your conversations with them.
  • Remove watchlist names (long-press) and delete journal entries; when you are signed in, the change follows you to your other phones.
  • Delete your account at any time: Recs → your account → Delete my account (or About → Delete your account), or on the web at mythoughtsaime.github.io/Privacy-Policy/delete-account.html. The account is shared with My Entertainment and Our Timeline, so it is deleted there too.
  • Block or report anyone from their name under Friends, or report a recommendation or a reply inside its conversation.
  • Sign out at any time; what is on the phone stays on the phone.
  • Ask for a copy of your data or a correction, or for help deleting your account, by writing from the email address you signed in with to mythoughtsaime@gmail.com.

You have rights under South Africa’s Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), and under the GDPR if you are in the EU or UK, including access, correction, deletion and objection. You can complain to South Africa’s Information Regulator or your local data-protection authority.

Recommendation rules

Before your first friend request or recommendation you are asked to agree to these rules. A recommendation is one friend’s opinion to another.

  • Be respectful. No harassment, hate, threats or personal attacks.
  • No market manipulation: no pump-and-dump, coordinated buying or selling, or false claims meant to move a price.
  • No inside information, and nothing you are not allowed to share.
  • No spam, advertising, referral links or paid promotion.
  • Don’t impersonate anyone or post anyone’s personal information.
  • Recommendations are opinions, not advice — don’t present them as guaranteed outcomes, and never ask a friend for money or to invest through you.
  • No links. Notes and replies with web links are refused.

Reporting and moderation

You can only be recommended stocks by people you accepted as friends. Report a person, a recommendation or a reply with a reason; block someone to stop all contact. Limits stop flooding: 20 waiting friend requests, 30 recommendations and 300 replies a day. The developer reviews every report and can remove content and accounts that break these rules. To raise anything else, write to mythoughtsaime@gmail.com.
