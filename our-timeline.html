Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-10-15. This policy covers the Our Timeline app. The developer, MyThoughtsAIme Apps, is responsible for your information.

Kept on this phone only

Your Hindsight answers, what you make of the AI's lines, your thoughts, your odds on the unknowns, your verdicts on the science-fiction lessons, which letters you've read and which conversations you've opened are stored on this phone and never sent anywhere. Your thoughts are never posted.

If you sign in to recommend

  • Email address — to send you a one-time sign-in code. It is never shown to other people.
  • Public name (handle) — how friends find you and see you. Anyone signed in can search for it, but only people you accept can send you anything. It is the same account and name as in the developer's other apps (My Investments, the Investment Playbook and My Entertainment), if you use them.
  • Friend requests and friends — who asked whom, when, and whether it was accepted.
  • Recommendations — the tip you chose, the friend you sent it to, your note if you wrote one, and the date.
  • Replies — the text and the date.
  • Reports — what you reported (a person, a recommendation or a reply), the reason, anything you added, and a copy of the reported words, so the developer can review it even if the sender deletes it.
  • Blocks — whom you blocked. Private to you.
  • When you agreed to the rules.

Nothing you send is public. A recommendation and its replies can be read only by the two friends in that conversation. Reports are read only by the developer, to moderate.

Technical data

The app checks Expo's update service for new versions, which sees the app version and the request's IP address, and the download buttons in About check GitHub, where the install files are kept. Links you open go to their own websites.

What the app does not do

  • No advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and no selling or renting of your information.
  • No access to your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Who processes it, and where

Supabase
Sign-in, sign-in emails, public names, friend requests, recommendations, replies, reports and blocks — EU (Ireland).
Expo (EAS Update)
Delivers app updates — United States.

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Database rules let people send only as themselves, only to accepted friends, one friend at a time, and only the two people in a conversation can read it. Nothing sent can be edited — only deleted.

How long it is kept

Account data, friends, recommendations and replies are kept until you delete them or delete your account, which removes everything linked to it at once. Reports are kept while they are reviewed, and as long as needed to keep the service safe. Earlier versions had a public Social tab; it is closed, and anything left from it is no longer shown and goes when you delete your account. What is on your phone stays until you delete it or remove the app.

Your choices and rights

  • Decline or ignore any friend request; nothing can reach you from someone you haven't accepted.
  • Remove a friend at any time: neither of you can send new recommendations or replies.
  • Block anyone: they can't send you requests, recommendations or replies, their conversations are hidden from you, and they stop being your friend. They aren't told.
  • Report a person, a recommendation or a reply from inside the app.
  • Delete any reply you wrote, or a whole conversation (for both of you).
  • Sign out at any time; everything on the phone stays.
  • Delete your account at any time: Recommendations → Delete my account (or About), or on the web (mythoughtsaime.github.io/Privacy-Policy/delete-account.html). It removes your account and everything linked to it, here and in the developer's other apps, which share it.
  • Ask for a copy of your data or a correction, or for help deleting your account, by writing from the email address you signed in with to mythoughtsaime@gmail.com.

You have rights under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), and under the GDPR if you are in the EU or UK, including access, correction, deletion and objection. You can complain to South Africa's Information Regulator or your local data-protection authority.

Children

The app is meant for adults (18 and over) and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes and the new version appears here.

Recommendation rules

Before your first recommendation or reply you are asked to agree to these rules.

  • Recommend only to people you know, and only what you think will help them. One friend at a time — never bulk or chain messages.
  • Be kind. No harassment, hate, threats, bullying or pressure — and take no for an answer.
  • No spam, advertising, referral codes, paid promotion or links.
  • No sexual content, no fake images of real people, no scams and nothing illegal.
  • Don't impersonate anyone or share anyone's personal information.

Moderation

Anyone can decline a request, remove a friend, block someone or report a person, a recommendation or a reply. Reports go to the developer with a copy of what was sent; the developer reviews them and can remove content and accounts that break these rules. For anything else, write to mythoughtsaime@gmail.com.
